➡️ **Benefit**: Provides a CRM-like system inside Notion, ensuring no lead is lost and follow-ups are personalized.

- **Availability Tracking**: Client availability fields make it easy to match schedules with agents.  
➡️ **Benefit**: Reduces manual scheduling, accelerates response time, and improves client satisfaction.
